How the Scrum Master runs the daily Como o scrum master conduz a daily Comment le Scrum Master anime la daily
  1. Opens the Kanban board and shares the screen before the daily starts. Abre o quadro Kanban e compartilha a tela antes de a daily começar. Ouvre le tableau Kanban et partage l'écran avant le début de la daily.

  3. Keeps the daily within 15 minutes and moves long discussions to after it. Mantém a daily em até 15 minutos e leva discussões longas para depois. Tient la daily en 15 minutes et reporte les longues discussions à après.
